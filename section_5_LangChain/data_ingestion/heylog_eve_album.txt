HEYLOG - EVE (FULL ALBUM, 2024)
=================================
Debut album by heylog. 7 tracks, 25 minutes. Alt-pop / emo / independent.
The album is approached biblically, focused on the figure of Eve.

============================================================
TRACK 1: GRAVEL
============================================================
LYRICS:
[Instrumental]
[Outro] (heylog)

INTERPRETATION:
Gravel opens the album as a purely instrumental ambient intro.
It builds atmosphere off the very first seconds - a soft, quiet
bed of reverb and textures that sets the to mood of what follows.
There are no words; it functions like a musical palate cleanser,
letting the album bloom out of silence.

unreachable.

INTERPRETATION:
The album's closing statement. An idyllic forest hike that
sours into psychological unease. The narrator seeks refuge in
nature to escape people, expectations, and his own relentless
thoughts - wanting to live off-grid in total isolation. But
he is ashamed of merely hiding instead of facing his problems,
and his paranoia spirals: deer that won't run, branches cracking,
a growing conviction that he is not alone. The recurring
"cover up all the shame like Adam and Eve" ties the whole album's
biblical thread together - hiding what feels wrong, ashamed of
oneself, reaching for an escape that never quite arrives.
